QUALIFICATION

Nobody goes home after the group stage

All six teams reach the playoffs. Where you finish in the group only decides which door you come in through:

  • 1st – 4th start in the Upper Bracket
  • 5th – 6th start in the Lower Bracket

Finishing high is worth real protection, not just pride — but finishing 5th or 6th is not an elimination.

FINAL PLACINGS

Where every team finishes

1st — Champion Winner of the Grand Final
2nd Loser of the Grand Final
3rd Loser of the Lower Bracket Final
4th Loser of the Lower Bracket Semifinal
5th – 6th Losers of the two Lower Bracket Quarterfinals

RESULTS

Where the scores come from

Admins record every round in the official tracker, and this site is generated straight from it — standings, tiebreakers and the bracket are all recalculated from the raw scores, never typed in by hand.

The footer of every page shows when the results were last updated. If a match you played is not showing yet, it simply has not been published — give it a moment.
